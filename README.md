## Giulia Ottino

Botanist working on plant taxonomy and biodiversity data. I build R tools that turn herbarium specimens, nomenclature and forest-plot records into material a taxonomist can work with.

CNPq DTI-B fellow in botany at the National Institute for Amazonian Research (INPA), Manaus. MSc in Botany, Rio de Janeiro Botanical Garden (JBRJ).

### Software

- **[forplotR](https://github.com/DBOSlab/forplotR)** — author and maintainer. Management and visualisation of permanent forest plot data: interactive maps of tagged trees, collection-balance reports, voucher folders, and herbarium label spreadsheets for JABOT and BRAHMS. Reads ForestPlots.net and MONITORA field formats.
- **[jabotR](https://github.com/DBOSlab/jabotR)** — co-author. R interface to the JABOT plant specimen collections of the Rio de Janeiro Botanical Garden: Darwin Core archives, coverage and gap analyses against Flora e Funga do Brasil.
- **Rtypus** — nomenclatural evidence for plant and fungal taxonomic revisions, compiled from WFO, IPNI, GBIF, POWO, Index Fungorum, MycoBank, Flora e Funga do Brasil and JABOT/REFLORA, with the provenance of every assertion. Pre-release.

Example output from forplotR: [interactive plot map, MONITORA format](https://giuliaottino.github.io/pages_MONITORA_forplotR/) · [ForestPlots.net / RAINFOR format](https://giuliaottino.github.io/html_RAINFOR_forplotR/)

### Sites I build and maintain

- **[tsiinohiiwiida.net](https://tsiinohiiwiida.net)** — Tsiino Hiiwiida, an Amazônia +10 project documenting the plant and fungal diversity of the Upper Rio Negro.
- **[redec2.net](https://redec2.net)** — RedeC2, a research network of permanent vegetation plots in the Caatinga and Cerrado.
